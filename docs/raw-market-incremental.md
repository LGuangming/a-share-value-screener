# 2026+ incremental raw market archive

Five official source datasets are appended under each dataset's year/month/day prefix as immutable verified Parquet files, with a per-day complete-market manifest under _incremental/market. Latest-day listed stock names are archived as observed snapshots only, not historical ST certification. Trading session evidence is recorded separately. The workflow is independent of market publication, shares the data pipeline lock to limit API contention, and triggers on a successful market-refresh run's actual database-recorded trading date; skipped and failed runs do not archive unready dates. Manual date-range backfill is bounded to 31 calendar days; partitions are reused and verified. Existing historical monthly files and production readers are unchanged. Financial statement source snapshots and historical ST are separate; this workflow does not claim full 2026 PIT financial or historical ST coverage. A complete current-year backfill requires running bounded date ranges; daily files are not a completed-year monthly manifest. The 2020–2025 migration catalog remains a historical migration receipt, not an index of future daily partitions.

## Current-year chart access and backfill

The workflow can backfill all completed 2026 trading sessions, latest month first, with daily checkpoints. It publishes content-addressed monthly reader snapshots of OHLCV and adjustment factors only from complete verified daily manifests; other raw datasets stay in daily partitions. Readers expose partial-year coverage, and the current-year chart is enabled. Daily and old reader snapshot objects are retained pending a separate retention audit. PostgreSQL daily_prices and compact price_history are not pruned by this workflow. Maintain sufficient compact closes for 50/90-session calculations before considering a rolling full-quote retention policy.
